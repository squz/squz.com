Privacy Policy – MultiMaze 2

Effective Date: June 29, 2026

MultiMaze 2 by Squz is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy describes how the app handles information.

Information We Collect

MultiMaze 2 collects no personal information. The app runs entirely offline and transmits no data about you off your device. There is no Squz account, no Squz server, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

Information Stored on Your Device

Your game progress — level completion, unlocked packs, and best times — is saved locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

In-App Purchases

MultiMaze 2 offers optional in-app purchases to unlock additional level packs, and a "Restore Purchases" option. These are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. We never see, receive, or store your name, payment details, or Apple ID — Apple handles the transaction and tells the app only whether a pack is owned. Apple's handling of purchase information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, physical location, photos, contacts, device identifiers, or any other personally identifiable information. The app contains no advertising, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party tracking or analytics SDKs. No data is shared with advertisers or any third party.

No Accounts or Server Communication

MultiMaze 2 does not require an account, internet connection, or sign-in. There are no user profiles and no cloud storage. The game works completely offline; the only network interaction is Apple's own App Store purchase and restore flow described above.

Children's Privacy

MultiMaze 2 does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app is suitable for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, leave a review on the App Store or get in touch via squz.com.
